# Governance

## Who decides

HTML-Template has one maintainer, [@VectoDE](https://github.com/VectoDE). The maintainer reviews and merges pull requests, sets the roadmap, cuts releases and has the final say on any open question. Contributors and reporters take part through issues, discussions and pull requests, as [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) describes.

## How decisions are recorded

Decisions that affect scope, design or process are posted in the "Decisions" category of the repository's discussions, with the reasoning and the date. Smaller choices are settled in the pull request or issue where they come up.

## Becoming a maintainer

Anyone who has made several accepted contributions over time and has shown good judgement in reviews can be invited by the maintainer. The invitation comes with write access. Release and settings access would be granted step by step. No such process is set up yet.

## If the maintainer is unavailable

This is a known gap. There is only one person with admin rights to the repository, the Cloudflare account and the release process. If the maintainer is away for a long time, nobody else can merge, release or change settings. No second person is set up to take over yet. Adding one is the way to close this gap, and it is not planned for any date.
